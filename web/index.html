How it works

It only ever reacts. Nothing goes out cold.

01

Connect your account

Link your Instagram professional account through Meta's official login. We ask for the two permissions needed to read and reply to DMs — nothing else.

02

A fan messages first

The assistant waits for someone to open the conversation, then replies inside Instagram's 24-hour window. It never messages people who didn't message you.

03

It discloses, then helps

The first reply says it's an AI assistant. It answers the usual questions in your tone and drops your link only when someone actually asks for it.

04

You take over anytime

Someone types "human", or you just want to jump in — automation pauses for that thread and a real person carries it from there.

The honest bit

It tells them it's a bot.
Ask if it's real — it says no.
Type stop — it's gone for good.

Hiding a bot behind a creator's face is deceptive, and it's against Instagram's rules. So we don't. The disclosure is the product.

Guardrails

Built to live inside the platform rules.

Inbound only

No cold outreach, no bulk sends, no follower scraping. The assistant answers conversations users start.

PG-13 replies

The assistant keeps messages non-explicit. Instagram prohibits sexual content in DMs — the guardrails hold that line.

Minimal data

We keep your scoped user ID, opt-out status and message counts. Message content isn't stored past drafting a reply.

Deletion on request

Fans and creators can wipe every trace of their data. Requests are honoured within 30 days.

Your content and the link you configure are your responsibility. Promoting adult content or services through Instagram DMs can breach Instagram's rules regardless of this tool — see the acceptable-use terms.

Questions
Does it pretend to be me?

No. The first automated message in every conversation states that it's an AI assistant acting on your behalf, and it confirms it's a bot whenever asked. That's a hard rule, not a setting.

Will this get my account banned?

It's designed to operate within the Instagram Platform Terms — inbound-only, disclosed, opt-out honoured, inside the 24-hour window. What it can't do is make an underlying use compliant: if the link you share solicits adult services, that carries risk regardless of this tool. We're upfront about that.

Which AI does it use?

Any standard, paid, OpenAI-compatible provider you choose — OpenAI, Anthropic, and others. No scraping of chat apps, no throwaway accounts.

Can a real person still reply?

Always. A fan can ask for a human, or you can take any thread over manually — automation pauses for that conversation immediately.

What does it store about my fans?

An Instagram-scoped ID, whether they opted out, and timestamps. Message text is used to draft the reply and then dropped. Full detail in the privacy policy.
