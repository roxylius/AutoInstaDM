Data Deletion

Last updated: 5 September 2026

This page explains how to have data that AutoInstaDM (operated by [Company Legal Name]) holds about you deleted. It applies both to people who message a participating creator and to creators who connect their account.

1. What we hold

For someone who messages a creator, we store very little:

  • your Instagram-scoped user ID (a per-app identifier from Meta);
  • whether you have opted out of automated replies or asked for a human;
  • the timestamp of your most recent message and aggregate message counts.

Message text is used only to generate a reply and is not stored afterwards, beyond short-lived processing and standard server logs. Full detail is in the Privacy Policy.

2. How to request deletion

Use whichever is easiest — any one of them removes everything we hold about you:

  • Message the creator's Instagram account the word DELETE (or STOP). This is handled automatically.
  • Remove the app via Meta. In Instagram, go to Settings → Website permissions / Apps and websites, remove AutoInstaDM, and Meta sends us a deletion request for your data automatically.
  • Email us at [Contact Email] from any address, describing which Instagram account you messaged from.

Creators can request deletion of their configuration and access tokens by emailing us or disconnecting the app.

3. What happens next

  • Automated requests (DELETE / app removal) are processed immediately.
  • Email requests are completed within 30 days; we may ask one question to confirm which data is yours.
  • We delete your conversation state, opt-out record, and analytics entries, and remove any queued messages. Backups roll off within their normal retention cycle.
  • We'll confirm by the same channel you contacted us on.

4. Automated deletion endpoint (for Meta)

AutoInstaDM also exposes a machine-to-machine data-deletion callback that Meta calls when a user removes the app: https://[your-app-host]/webhook/deletion-callback. It verifies Meta's signed request, deletes the user's data, and returns a confirmation code and status URL.
