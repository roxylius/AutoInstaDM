Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 September 2026

This policy describes how [Company Legal Name] ("we", "us") operates AutoInstaDM ("Service"): an AI assistant that replies to Instagram direct messages that users send to a participating creator's Instagram professional account ("Creator").

1. Who is the data controller

For people who message a Creator ("Message Senders"), the Creator and [Company Legal Name] act as joint controllers for the limited processing described here. For Creators who sign up, [Company Legal Name] is the controller. Contact: [Contact Email].

2. Data we process

From Message Senders

  • Instagram-scoped user ID (IGSID): a per-app identifier Meta assigns you. It cannot identify you outside this app.
  • Message content you send to the Creator: used transiently to generate a reply. It is sent to our AI provider for that purpose and is not stored by us after the reply is generated, aside from short-lived processing queues and standard server logs.
  • Conversation state: whether you opted out, whether you asked for a human, and the timestamp of your most recent message (to respect Instagram's 24-hour messaging window).
  • Aggregate counts: number of messages received and sent.

From Creators

  • Account identifiers and the access token Meta issues for the connected Instagram professional account.
  • Configuration you provide (display name, business description, a single link, opt-out preferences).
  • Contact and billing details where applicable.

We do not knowingly process special-category data, and we don't want you to send it. We do not collect payment card numbers through the Service.

3. Why we process it (legal bases)

  • To provide the Service — replying to messages you chose to send (contract / legitimate interests).
  • To comply with platform rules and law — AI disclosure, opt-out handling, the 24-hour window, data-deletion obligations (legal obligation / legitimate interests).
  • Security and abuse prevention — verifying webhook signatures, rate limiting, logging (legitimate interests).

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use message content to train AI models.

4. Sharing

  • AI provider: message text and the assistant's instructions are sent to an OpenAI-compatible API provider selected by the operator (e.g. OpenAI, Anthropic) to generate replies, under that provider's terms.
  • Meta Platforms: we exchange data with Instagram's API to receive and send messages.
  • Infrastructure providers: hosting and an optional Redis cache, acting as processors.
  • Legal: where required by law or to protect rights and safety.

5. Retention

Conversation state and aggregate counts are kept for up to 45 days after your last interaction, then deleted or anonymised. Server logs containing metadata are retained for up to [30] days. Message content is not retained beyond transient processing.

6. Your rights and how to delete your data

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing, and to data portability. To exercise them:

  • Message Senders: reply DELETE or STOP to the Creator's account, use the form at /webhook/data-deletion on the Creator's Service instance, or email [Contact Email].
  • Via Meta: removing the app or using Meta's data-deletion tools triggers our deletion callback automatically.
  • Creators: email us or disconnect the app; we delete configuration and tokens.

We complete verified deletion requests within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

7. International transfers

Our providers may process data in the United States and other countries. Where required, transfers rely on Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent safeguards.

8. Children

The Service is not directed to anyone under 18. If we learn that data from a minor has been processed, we delete it. Creators must not use the Service to interact with people they know or believe to be minors.

9. Security

We verify inbound webhook signatures, restrict tokens to the minimum scopes, rate-limit requests, and limit data retention. No system is perfectly secure.

10. Changes

We will post changes here and update the date above. Material changes will be communicated to Creators.
